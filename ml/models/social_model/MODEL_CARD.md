## Purpose
Detects scam-induced payments (digital arrest, fake support refund, investment task), where the real customer is talked into paying. Output `social_eng_score` feeds `meta_model` and triggers the Scam Coach (spec 01 F1).

## Training data
Simulated UPI data, time split 70/15/15; target = the three scam scenarios. 2,596 of 5,129 STEP_UP-range training rows got simulated Scam Coach answers (418 with a red flag). Training data hash `6ff17ea28596fdfc`.

## Features
10 LightGBM inputs: `payee_is_new`, `payee_account_age_days`, `payee_unique_senders_24h`, `payee_reputation`, `payee_report_count`, `ctx_unknown_call`, `ctx_screen_share`, `ctx_call_long`, `coach_red_flags`, `amount_ratio_avg`.
Monotone constraints: turning on a call, screen share, long call or a coach red flag can never lower the score (spec 01 F2).

## Limitations
- Trained on simulated UPI data: scam signatures are cleaner than real life, so simulated scores overstate real performance (the IEEE-CIS benchmark is the realistic reference).
- Coach answers are simulated; real answer patterns must be collected (T5.4).
- Scams without call, screen-share or new-payee signals are hard to see.
