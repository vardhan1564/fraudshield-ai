## Purpose
Scores every account in the payment graph as a likely mule (spec 01 F3). The worker's graph_refresh writes it to Redis `graph:{account_id}` as `graph_score` every 5 minutes; the payer's score is an input to `meta_model`, and accounts above the threshold get the `gnn` flag in the Graph Explorer and can form rings.

## Training data
Simulator worlds with seeds [101, 102] (training) and 103 (early stopping, threshold), never the served dataset (seed 42). 60 30-day graph snapshots, one every 2 days: 591,425 account rows, 9,581 of them mules.
Confirmed fraud offline = payees with >= 2 earlier reports (online: labelled cases, confirmed rings and payees).

## Features
GraphSAGE, 2 x SAGEConv (64 hidden), on account, payee, device and IP nodes. 18 node features (standardised): `type_A`, `type_P`, `type_D`, `type_I`, `graph_degree`, `graph_pagerank`, `hops_to_fraud`, `community_fraud_ratio`, `in_count`, `out_count`, `in_amount`, `out_amount`, `distinct_senders`, `senders_24h`, `forwarded_share`, `account_age`, `device_accounts`, `young_incoming`.

## Metrics
| Where | Ring recall | Mule recall | Ordinary accounts flagged |
|---|---|---|---|
| Served dataset, 1 h after each ring's last payment | 100.0% | 99.5% | 0.0% |
| Served dataset, 6 h after each ring's first payment | 100.0% | 94.9% | 0.0% |
| Validation world (all snapshots) | n/a | 100.0% | 0.0% |

A ring counts as found when at least half its accounts score above the threshold. Target ring recall >= 85%. Validation PR-AUC 1.0000.

## Thresholds
- `gnn` flag when graph_score > 0.9403: the highest threshold keeping the recall reached at 1% FPR on validation.

## Limitations
- Trained on simulated UPI data: scam signatures are cleaner than real life, so simulated scores overstate real performance (the IEEE-CIS benchmark is the realistic reference).
- Simulated rings are textbook (fan-in, fast forwarding, shared phones), so the rule detectors alone already find them; real mule networks hide better.
- Not pre-trained on Elliptic (spec 03 §3 lists it): the simulator graph was enough to meet the target.

## Fairness results
Not measured on its own: fairness is checked on the final risk_score.
