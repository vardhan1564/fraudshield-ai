# Model card: txn_model

> Generated by `python -m ml.pipelines.tune --dataset simulated`. Do not edit by hand;
> re-run the command to refresh the numbers.

## Purpose
Scores every payment (UPI, card, wallet) for general fraud patterns. Its output is
`txn_score`, one of the inputs to `meta_model`, which makes the final APPROVE / STEP_UP /
HOLD decision. Every score is explained with SHAP reasons (spec 03 §7).

## Training data
- Simulated UPI data (`ml/simulator`, seed 42): 84,779 payments after a 14-day warm-up (features need history).
- Time split 70/15/15: train 59,345, validation 12,717, test 12,717 rows. Fraud rate in test 2.3%.
- Training data hash: `561acbaacb8aae30`.

## Limitations
- Trained on simulated data. The scam signatures are cleaner than real life, so the near-perfect simulated scores overstate real performance; the IEEE-CIS benchmark is the realistic reference.
- Fraud patterns not in the simulator (new scam types) are not learned.
- Graph and Scam Coach features are 0 until phase 5, so mule detection relies on payee and velocity signals only.
- `payee_reputation` ignores confirmed cases and the graph flag until T5.5.
