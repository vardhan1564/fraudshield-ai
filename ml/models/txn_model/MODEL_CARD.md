## Purpose
Scores every payment (UPI, card, wallet) for general fraud patterns. Output `txn_score` feeds `meta_model`, which makes the APPROVE / STEP_UP / HOLD decision. Its TreeSHAP values are the `top_reasons` of every decision.

## Limitations
- Trained on simulated UPI data: scam signatures are cleaner than real life, so simulated scores overstate real performance (the IEEE-CIS benchmark is the realistic reference).
- Scam types the simulator does not generate are not learned.
- Graph and Scam Coach features are 0 until phase 5.
- `payee_reputation` ignores confirmed cases and the graph flag until T5.5.
